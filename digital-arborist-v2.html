08 — Before it goes liveDigital Arborist
The parts that decide whether anyone trusts it

Three things
we will not skip.

Reporting
is not risk

Affluent streets call 311 more often. We audit P1 recall by ward and income decile. A tree's priority must never depend on how many people complained about it.

The model
can abstain

Below 0.85 confidence it says so and hands the request to an arborist. Roughly 17% of intake. Every override is logged with the image and becomes a training label.

It recommends.
It never closes

A person dispatches, always. That line is what makes the audit trail defensible on the day a limb comes down anyway — and it will.
